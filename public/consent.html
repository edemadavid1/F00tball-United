Football United Permission Form

Dear Guardian,

Your child has indicated their wish to participate in the Football United programme managed by Hillsong Church UK with support of Sport England. Please read the information on this form, sign it and have the young person bring it with them on their first session of Football United.

Key information:

Football United is hosted and organised by Hillsong UK, a local church in London for asylum seeking and refugee youth of 14 – 19 years old with a junior and senior session. The aim of Football United is to engage, equip and empower young people in our local community in their football skills, and at the same time build their confidence and help overcome their fears in all areas of life. Anyone is welcome, it is not a religious programme, it is purely an informal night to play and learn football skills.

All our coaches and leaders have been DBS checked and have had extensive safeguarding training. Our coaching team has F.A. Coaching Badges to ensure that the sessions are run with excellence.

There are no costs to participate in Football United. Only requirement is for the young person to bring their signed permission form to the first night. The permission form will be valid for the whole term and ongoing participation in Football United programme.

WHEN:

Every Friday night with a junior session 6:30-8pm and a senior session 8-9:30pm

Junior Session: 14–16 yrs (6:30pm–8pm)
Senior Session: 17–19 yrs (8pm–9:30pm)
Cost: Free of charge

ADDRESS:

Trinity School, Shirley Park, Croydon, CR9 7AT

(Tramstop Sandilands or Bus 119, 194, 198, 466 stop: Trinity School)

WHAT DO YOU NEED:

  • Your signed permission slip for your first time. No permission, no play
  • Any football kit you might have (No boots with studs allowed)
  • Water and/or snacks
